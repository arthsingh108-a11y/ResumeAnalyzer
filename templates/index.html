How It Works

1. Text Extraction & Parsing

The uploaded PDF is processed in Python using PyPDF. All text content across pages is extracted into a normalized memory buffer without saving personal documents to disk.

2. ATS Formatting & Sections

Checks for essential resume sections (Education, Skills, Projects, Experience, Certifications) and basic ATS hygiene including email, phone number, and minimum word count.

3. Skill & Keyword Detection

The resume is tokenized and scanned using word-boundary regular expressions against core technical competencies and action-oriented engineering keywords.

4. Job Match Comparison

When a job posting is supplied, stopword-filtered keywords and required skills are extracted and cross-referenced to compute a tailored match score.

ATS Scoring Weights

Metric Weight Criteria
Technical Skills 40% Coverage of detected programming languages, frameworks, and databases
Standard Sections 20% Presence of Education, Skills, Projects, Experience, and Certifications
Action Keywords 20% Presence of engineering action verbs (develop, design, implement, deploy, optimize)
Projects & Experience 10% Dedicated sections demonstrating practical implementation
ATS Formatting 10% Contact information formatting, length criteria, and text readability
